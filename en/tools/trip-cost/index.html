Trip cost

What it costs to drive from A to B, side by side: the same trip in a city car or an SUV, on petrol or electric.

The sums are done in your browser, but to find addresses and roads this tool uses three OpenStreetMap services: Nominatim receives the addresses you type and the points you pick on the map, the FOSSGIS Valhalla routing server receives the start and destination coordinates, and the map itself comes from openstreetmap.org. Prices, choices and addresses are saved on this computer only.

Consumption and tolls are reasonable estimates, not type-approval figures: they are there to compare, not to count every cent.

Settings

1 Fuel prices Petrol, diesel and LPG update themselves every week; you can always type your own.

Petrol, diesel and LPG are the national average Italy reports to the European Commission every week for its Weekly Oil Bulletin, taxes included. CNG is not in the bulletin and electricity has no weekly official source: for those two the starting value is a rough one. Electricity, moreover, varies a lot between charging at home (about €0.30/kWh) and fast public chargers (€0.60–0.80/kWh).

Fuel

The plug-in starts with a full battery and switches to petrol when it runs out. The tolled km are the ones OpenStreetMap marks as paid; the amount is an estimate designed for Italy, an average rate for a class A car. Other countries have other rules (vignettes, toll-free motorways): there, untick the box below.
